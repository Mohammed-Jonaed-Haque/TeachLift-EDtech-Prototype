# TeachLift

### Mark less. Understand more.

**TeachLift** is an AI-assisted EdTech prototype designed to help educators move beyond simply marking student assessments.

Instead of stopping at whether an answer is right or wrong, TeachLift is designed to help identify **why a student may be struggling**, allow the educator to verify that diagnosis, recommend targeted follow-up learning, measure improvement, and communicate progress to parents.

> **AI assists. Educators decide.**

## The Problem

Educators spend significant time:

- reviewing assessments;
- identifying mistakes;
- writing individual feedback;
- creating follow-up activities; and
- communicating student progress.

However, knowing that a student answered a question incorrectly does not necessarily explain **why the student made the mistake** or what intervention will help them improve.

TeachLift was designed around three stakeholders:

### Educators

Reduce repetitive assessment and feedback work while providing clearer learning insights.

### Students

Receive personalised feedback that focuses on the misconception behind the mistake and what to work on next.

### Parents

Gain clearer visibility into learning progress, current strengths and areas requiring attention.

---

## Our Solution

TeachLift is designed around a six-stage learning loop:

### MARK → DIAGNOSE → VERIFY → TARGET → MEASURE → REPORT

### 1. Mark

TeachLift performs a simulated AI-assisted first-pass analysis using educator-provided assessment criteria, model answers and rubrics.

### 2. Diagnose

Instead of only identifying an incorrect answer, TeachLift identifies the potential **misconception or learning gap** behind the student's response.

### 3. Verify

AI does not make the final assessment decision.

The educator can:

- review the suggested mark;
- inspect the proposed misconception;
- review the reasoning;
- edit feedback;
- change the mark;
- approve the suggestion; or
- request manual review.

This maintains a **human-in-the-loop** assessment process.

### 4. Target

Once the educator confirms the learning gap, TeachLift recommends targeted follow-up activities based on that specific weakness.

### 5. Measure

TeachLift tracks performance before and after the intervention so educators can see whether the student has improved.

### 6. Report

Student progress can then be communicated through a simplified parent-facing progress report.

# Example Learning Journey

The prototype follows a fictional Year 8 Mathematics student named **Alex Chen**.

### Assessment Question

**Expand:**

`3(x + 4)`

### Student Answer

`3x + 4`

### TeachLift Analysis

**Suggested Mark:** 0/1

**Potential Learning Gap:** Distributive Law

The student multiplied `3 × x` but did not multiply the second term inside the bracket.

### Educator Verification

The educator reviews the AI suggestion and makes the final decision.

### Targeted Intervention

TeachLift then recommends questions focused specifically on the distributive law.

Examples:

- `4(x + 3)`
- `2(3x + 5)`
- `5(2x - 4)`

### Progress

Initial Assessment:

**45%**

↓

Learning Gap Identified

↓

Targeted Practice

↓

Follow-Up Assessment:

**72%**

↓

**Status: Improving**

This represents TeachLift's central idea:

> **Mistake → Intervention → Outcome**

# Key Features

## Educator Dashboard

Provides an overview of:

- active students;
- assessments requiring review;
- identified learning gaps;
- follow-up completion;
- recent assessments; and
- class-wide learning insights.

---

## AI-Assisted Assessment Review

Educators can inspect:

- student answers;
- suggested marks;
- potential misconceptions;
- AI reasoning;
- marking criteria;
- personalised feedback; and
- review status.

The educator remains responsible for the final decision.

---

## Misconception Detection

Rather than simply saying:

> "The answer is incorrect."

TeachLift is designed to answer:

> **"Why might this student have made this mistake?"**

This is one of the key differentiators of the concept.

---

## Targeted Follow-Up

TeachLift connects diagnosed learning gaps with targeted learning activities.

The educator can review the recommended work before assigning it to the student.

---

## Student Progress Tracking

Student profiles display:

- overall performance;
- topic-level strengths;
- areas requiring attention;
- interventions;
- follow-up completion; and
- before-and-after assessment results.

---

## Class Learning-Gap Analytics

TeachLift can also identify patterns across groups of students.

For example:

| Learning Gap | Students Affected |
|---|---:|
| Distributive Law | 29 |
| Factorising Expressions | 21 |
| Linear Equations | 14 |
| Index Laws | 9 |

This allows educators to identify when an issue may require a **class-level intervention**, rather than only individual feedback.

---

## Parent Progress Reports

Parent-facing reports translate assessment data into simple progress information such as:

- biggest improvement;
- current strengths;
- current learning focus;
- targeted activity completion; and
- next learning goal.

---

# Human-in-the-Loop AI

A central design principle of TeachLift is:

## AI assists. Educators decide.

TeachLift is not designed to autonomously determine final student grades.

The intended workflow is:

**AI Suggestion → Educator Review → Educator Decision**

The prototype makes this distinction visible throughout the assessment process.

---

# Prototype User Journey

The interactive demonstration follows this workflow:

```text
Educator Dashboard
        ↓
Open Assessment
        ↓
View AI First-Pass Analysis
        ↓
Identify Potential Learning Gap
        ↓
Educator Reviews & Verifies
        ↓
Assign Targeted Follow-Up
        ↓
View Student Progress
        ↓
Analyse Class-Wide Learning Gaps
        ↓
Generate Parent Progress Report
```

This demonstrates the complete TeachLift learning loop.

# Technology

The interactive prototype was developed using:

- **Base44** — rapid application prototyping
- AI-assisted product development
- Responsive web-based UI
- Simulated assessment and student data

The current version focuses on demonstrating the **product experience and workflow**, rather than implementing production AI models or school infrastructure.

---

# Prototype Scope

The current prototype does **not** implement:

- real AI assessment processing;
- real student records;
- real parent accounts;
- school-management-system integrations;
- payment processing;
- authentication infrastructure; or
- production email delivery.

All student names, assessment records and learning analytics displayed in the prototype are **fictional demonstration data**.

The project explored how AI could help reduce repetitive educator workload while maintaining meaningful educator oversight and improving the way assessment feedback leads to future learning.

TeachLift is not intended to replace educators.

It is designed to reduce repetitive work while keeping professional judgement with the educator.

> **Don't just identify the mistake. Understand it, act on it, and measure what happens next.**
